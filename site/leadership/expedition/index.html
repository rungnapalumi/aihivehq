Leadership development journey

From one day in the forest to 30 days of growth

The expedition is the centre of a five-stage journey. Human coaches lead it; AI helps organise the evidence and keep momentum afterwards.

1
Before ExpeditionLeadership Discovery

Participants set personal goals and context with their coach.

2
During ExpeditionBehavioral Evidence Collection

Coaches record decisions and observations as teams complete the missions.

3
After ExpeditionAI Leadership Report

Recorded decisions and coach notes are organised into a structured report.

4
Human CoachingGuided Reflection

A professional coach debriefs the report with each participant.

5
Next 30 DaysLeadership Action Plan

Small weekly actions applied back at work.

Continued with AIHive Coach

The expedition ends. The development doesn’t.

After the debrief, participants can continue with AIHive Coach, the AIHiveHQ coaching assistant. It can help a participant:

  • break the 30-day action plan into weekly steps,
  • reflect on real workplace situations using the expedition’s questions, and
  • prepare for follow-up sessions with their human coach.

Linking an expedition report to an AIHive Coach account is planned for a later phase. Today, AIHive Coach is available as a separate product and each user’s coaching conversations stay private to them.
